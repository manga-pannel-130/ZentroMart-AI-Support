# Two-minute demo recording plan

Record this after setting up your own Gnani key and testing the flow with your own voice. Use only the fictional practice records and your own recording. Keep the finished video under two minutes.

1. **0:00-0:15 — Start:** show the running ZentroMart support app and say that the customer's voice goes through Gnani STT and the same support agent handles the request.
2. **0:15-0:45 — Grounded answer:** ask a short question answered by a current policy, such as the Wallet refund time. Show the reply and the source ID used.
3. **0:45-1:15 — Follow-up:** ask a contextual follow-up without repeating the earlier detail. Show that the answer remains grounded and concise.
4. **1:15-1:50 — Handover or abstention:** ask an unsupported question or use a practice escalation scenario. Show that the agent says it does not know or creates a ticket, and only claims success after the tool confirms it.
5. **1:50-2:00 — Voice output:** play the Gnani TTS answer and briefly show the saved transcript, reply text, audio file, and retrieved section IDs from `sample_outputs/`.

Do not edit the agent's generated response before recording. Show genuine runs and retain the real output files.
